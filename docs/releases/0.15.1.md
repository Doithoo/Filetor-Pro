# Filetor Pro 0.15.1

- 修复从磁盘根目录比较、同步和搜索时的相对路径；不同子目录里的同名文件会保留各自的位置。
- 重复文件报告仅在移入废纸篓成功后更新副本数与可释放空间；关闭或切换报告不会被之前的扫描覆盖。
- 大目录切换选区时复用已排序的列表；切换选择后停止旧信息面板的大小计算。无冲突的同卷移动不再预先遍历文件夹。
- Spotlight 查询读取最多 3,000 个结果，可以中途取消。发布包不再包含仅供调试使用的本地符号。

## English

- Preserve relative paths when comparing, syncing or searching from the filesystem root.
- Keep reports tied to the active scan. Update duplicate counts and reclaimable space only after a successful move to Trash.
- Reuse sorted rows when selection changes, cancel obsolete inspector work, and skip directory sizing for conflict-free moves on the same volume.
- Stop Spotlight queries on cancellation or at 3,000 results. Keep debugging symbols separately from the download.
