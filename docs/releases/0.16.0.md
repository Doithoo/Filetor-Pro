# Filetor Pro 0.16.0

- 在应用窗口中按下鼠标左键或右键时播放轻微的点击声，悬停与滚动保持安静。
- 复制、移动完成后播放短提示音；移入废纸篓、永久删除和清空废纸篓使用另一种声音。批量操作只提示一次，取消或部分失败不会播放成功音。
- 在「设置 → 通用 → 音效」中可以分别关闭点击音效和文件操作音效。

## English

- Left and right mouse presses in app windows give a quiet click. Hovering and scrolling stay silent.
- Copying and moving files give a short completion sound; trashing, permanent deletion and emptying Trash use a distinct sound. A batch plays once; cancelled or partially failed operations do not play a success sound.
- Toggle mouse clicks and file operation sounds independently in Settings → General → Sound effects.
