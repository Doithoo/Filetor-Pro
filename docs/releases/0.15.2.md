# Filetor Pro 0.15.2

- 大批量同步在后台准备目标文件夹；同一位置只准备一次，慢磁盘不会因此阻塞窗口。可以从传输列表取消准备。
- 无法创建的目录只报告一次，其他目录继续同步。双向同步仍可一次撤销。
- 处理大批普通文件时，不再反复读取前面每个文件的属性来判断选区是否重叠。

## English

- Prepare sync destinations in the background, once per folder. Preparation is visible and cancellable in the transfer list.
- Report a failed destination once and continue with other folders. A two-way sync remains a single undo step.
- Avoid repeatedly reading file metadata when checking large selections of regular files for overlapping entries.
